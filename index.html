Beginner's Guide to Bouldering


Basic Rules
  • Use the same color holds
  • Finish with both hands on the final hold
  • Don't stand on the mat unless you are going to climb
Bouldering Equipment
  • Bouldering shoes
  • Climbing Chalk
  • Brush
Basic Techniques
  • Keep your arms straight
  • Use your legs
  • Flagging
